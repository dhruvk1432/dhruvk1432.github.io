IAQF Student Competition 2026 Winner

Cross-Currency Dynamics in Cryptocurrencies

I studied the March 2023 USDC de-peg using high-frequency Binance, Coinbase, and Kraken data, separating visible quote-currency fragmentation from true USD-adjusted arbitrage residuals.

The main result is a two-layer market story: adjusted BTC basis dislocations mean-reverted quickly, while the stablecoin reserve shock persisted and transmitted through liquidity, volatility, and executable arbitrage constraints.

Option Portfolio Construction · Pre-production Research

Option-Only Markowitz as Conditional Option Cashflow Engineering

Working paper on allocating only across listed calls and puts by treating options as premium-funded, expiring, state-contingent cashflows rather than ordinary asset return columns.

The framework maps each option into NAV-normalized cashflows, Greeks, conditional option-risk-premium forecasts, VIX settlement controls, implementation screens, and validation gates. The evidence supports disciplined pre-production research diagnostics, not a live-trading or broker-executed alpha claim.

Cross-Asset Portfolio Optimization · v1.0.1

Cross-Asset Portfolio Construction as Cashflow Engineering

Working paper on SPY-centered allocation that treats equities, futures, Treasuries, VIX futures, and SPY options as funded cashflow processes before optimization.

The evidence supports framework usefulness rather than deployable alpha: downside-aware cross-asset allocation improves the tested SPY path under the paper's conventions, while option convexity is conditional on premium, skew, hedge cost, execution cost, and realized state. The public page links to the current PDF, selected release figures, code, and reproduction commands.

Mutual Fund Performance Inference

Bootstrap Inference for Mutual Fund Alphas: Luck, Skill, and the Right Tail

Reproducible bootstrap analysis of a WRDS-derived mutual-fund panel from 1987 through 2025, testing whether apparent alpha winners remain exceptional after data-quality gates, Carhart factor adjustment, and joint zero-alpha inference.

The main result is that the best observed funds are statistically indistinguishable from luck under the cleaned sample, Carhart benchmark, and implemented bootstrap design.
