IAQF Student Competition 2026 Winner

Cross-Currency Dynamics in Cryptocurrencies

I studied the March 2023 USDC de-peg using high-frequency Binance, Coinbase, and Kraken data, separating visible quote-currency fragmentation from true USD-adjusted arbitrage residuals.

The main result is a two-layer market story: adjusted BTC basis dislocations mean-reverted quickly, while the stablecoin reserve shock persisted and transmitted through liquidity, volatility, and executable arbitrage constraints.

Option-Only Markowitz · Greek-Induced Covariance · Theory to Execution

Option-Only Portfolio Optimization with Greek-Induced Covariance and Conditional Risk Premia

Developed a portfolio theory for books built entirely from listed options, where classical Markowitz breaks down: premium weights replace the unit budget, covariance is induced through a Greek/state factor map plus regularized residual risk, expected returns come from structural risk-premium channels instead of noisy historical contract means, and the allocation is a conic maximum-Sharpe program whose feasible set carries gross and short premium, stress-loss, margin, and pre-trade liquidity budgets inside the optimizer rather than as after-the-fact accounting.

Then took the theory to exchange-realistic practice: contract-level spread/fee/slippage/margin/assignment costs built from historical market-hours CBBO quotes, volume-aware participation caps, exact VRO/SOQ VIX settlement, and whole-contract execution. The headline book — a locked 56-name equity-option universe plus VIX options — earns a full-cost net Sharpe of 1.63 (2.01 gross) with net Sortino 4.38, beats both a stock-Markowitz baseline on the same underlyings and a capped naive option book (+1.34 Sharpe versus naive, p < 0.001), and holds up out of sample: rolling 36-month refits at 1.27 net Sharpe, positive purged combinatorial cross-validation in both test designs, Monte Carlo resampled and refit fifth percentiles above 1.0, and a deflated Sharpe ratio of 0.996 after charging for every candidate examined. The smaller eight-name VIX book confirms the mechanism at 1.38 net. Displayed option depth supports the book at $1M NAV and refuses it at $5M, making it a viabl small size book strategy.

Cross-Asset Portfolio Optimization · Admission-Audit Rewrite

Cross-Asset Portfolio Construction as Cashflow Engineering

Capital-admission audit for a SPY-centered book that treats equities, futures, Treasuries, VIX futures, and SPY options as funded cashflow processes before optimization.

The main result is selective: the downside-score cross-asset book is admitted, option sleeves are qualified as conditional downside protection, ML forecasts are rejected as a capital rule, and full diagnostics stay in the appendix for auditability.

Mutual Fund Performance Inference

Bootstrap Inference for Mutual Fund Alphas: Luck, Skill, and the Right Tail

Reproducible bootstrap analysis of a WRDS-derived mutual-fund panel from 1987 through 2025, testing whether apparent alpha winners remain exceptional after data-quality gates, Carhart factor adjustment, and joint zero-alpha inference.

The main result is that the best observed funds are statistically indistinguishable from luck under the cleaned sample, Carhart benchmark, and implemented bootstrap design.

  • ERCOT Power-Market Forecasting Platform

    A point-in-time, leakage-free forecasting platform for ERCOT power markets that corrects the grid operator's own published day-ahead forecasts for wind, temperature, and load. Hub-pooled quantile gradient-boosting models learn the systematic error of the public forecast under a strict D-1 09:30 decision-time contract, so no realized weather, actuals, or post-decision revisions ever enter a feature. Skill is verified out-of-sample on both monthly and quarterly walk-forward folds, with seasonal-breadth and coverage gates, deterministic hash-matched reruns, and a sealed holdout that was never touched.
